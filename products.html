Chocolate Notes

  • Storage: keep bars in a cool, dry place (15–18°C), away from sunlight.
  • Shelf life: best enjoyed within 6 months of the date on the wrapper.
  • Allergy note: contains milk. Special Edition also contains hazelnuts.
  • Gift packs: add a greeting card or ribbon from the Contact page.
  • Tip: let the chocolate rest at room temperature for a few minutes for the best flavour.
